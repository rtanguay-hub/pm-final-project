# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for the next 12 months, we will officially become the destination for "meh" content. That will force us towards a given price point for our service which will likely impact our bottom line as less customers subscribe and those who do subscribe can only be acquired at a lower price.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Users are likely searching online, watching the cottage industry of Youtubers who try to help people find good quality movies on our site, or just going to competitors who cost more and have less quantity of content but are always high quality.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must solve our decreasing revenues and customer counts by helping customers quickly and easily engage with our high quality content.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For our aficionado customers, we will prioritize quality content engagement over volume of content.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** Yes. The fact that we will have less subscribers at a lower price point is very concerning.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** The fact that there's an entire cottage industry means its a systemic problem.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** I think it removes it because engagement is broader than just "build a better search tool." Engagement means how the aficionado customers consume the content which inherently means they need to find it but also maybe they want certain features when consuming it too.
