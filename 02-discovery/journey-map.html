The Mood-Driven Seeker

An engaged subscriber who knows the feeling they want from tonight's viewing but rarely knows a specific title. Goal: describe the mood and get a short list of good matches within minutes.
StrategyRestore brand authority through human-led curation, not mass-market scale.
Value PropFor aficionados, quality of engagement over volume of content.

Stage 1

Arrive: Set Tonight's Mood

User ActionOpens the app to a calm screen asking “What are you in the mood for?”
Internal StateTired · Wary Braced for another long scroll.
Pain Point AddressedA “loud and new” home screen; blaring autoplay trailers. UXR-09 · UXR-05 · BUG-1077
  • Silent mood prompt replaces autoplay → user starts focused, not overwhelmed.
  • Mood tiles like “Quiet Sunday” → one-tap entry with no title needed.
Stage 2

Describe: Speak the Vibe

User ActionTypes “slow quiet French drama” or taps “Book Club Pick.”
Internal StateHopeful · Doubtful Expects search to fail again.
Pain Point AddressedSearch that only works with exact titles. UXR-07 · BUG-1080
  • Natural-language search reads mood, pace and language → results match intent.
  • Curator-tagged catalog powers results → hidden gems surface instead of franchise blockbusters.
Stage 3

Decide: Trust the Shortlist

User ActionReviews 3–5 picks, each with a short curator note on why it fits.
Internal StateRelieved · Guided The choice feels manageable.
Pain Point AddressedChoice overload; repetitive, genre-only recommendations. UXR-01 · UXR-12 · UXR-02 · BUG-1091
  • Shortlist capped at five → decision takes minutes, not twenty.
  • Curator's “why this film” note → trust moves from friends to the platform.
  • Picks span genres and eras → user treated as a person, not a genre.
Stage 4

Watch & Return: A Good Evening, Remembered

User ActionWatches, rates how well it fit the mood, and signs up for a weekly curated digest.
Internal StateSatisfied · Loyal Feels understood; curious for next time.
Pain Point AddressedComfort rewatching; leaving for curated competitors. UXR-03 · UXR-04
  • One-tap “Right mood?” rating → future picks sharpen to personal taste.
  • Weekly hand-picked digest → a reason to return without browsing.
  • Saved picks sync across devices → a discovery is never lost between screens.
Today20 minutes of scrolling → app closed, nothing watched (UXR-01)
FutureUnder 5 minutes from opening the app to a film that fits the mood

Competitive Advantages over the Manual Workaround

1 · Expert advice whenever they want it

Curators' picks are there every night, inside the app, right when the user is deciding.

vs. asking a friend, which depends on someone being available and having good taste

2 · Every pick can be played now

Every curated pick is in our catalog and available to watch immediately.

vs. Google and Reddit lists, which don't know our catalog and lead to dead ends

3 · It gets better with use

Each mood rating improves the next shortlist, so the platform becomes more valuable and harder to leave.

vs. comfort rewatching, which narrows what a user watches over time
